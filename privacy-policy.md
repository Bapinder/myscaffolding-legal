# Privacy Policy — My Scaffolding Ltd (Scaffolding app)

**Last updated:** 25 August 2026
**Data controller:** My Scaffolding Ltd
**Contact:** myscaffoldingltd13@outlook.com

This policy covers the **Scaffolding** app for iPhone and Android. It is written to be read,
not to be waved at a regulator, so it says plainly what the app holds and where it goes.

---

## What the app is for

The app records scaffolding jobs: who a scaffold is for, where it is, what it is worth, when it
went up and when it came down. It is used by the staff of My Scaffolding Ltd, not by the public.

## What the app stores

Everything below is entered by the person using the app, or captured when they tap a button
asking for it.

| What | Why | How it is captured |
|---|---|---|
| **Client name, site address, contact name and phone number** | To identify whose scaffold it is and who to ring | Typed in |
| **Crew member names** | So a job shows who did what | Typed in |
| **Job value and weekly hire rate** | To track hire earned | Typed in |
| **Photographs of scaffolds and sites** | Evidence of what was erected | Camera or photo library, only when you choose a photo |
| **GPS coordinates of a site or of the yard** | To pin a site and show its distance from the yard | Device location, only when you tap "Use my location" |
| **A signature drawn on screen** | On the handover certificate | Drawn with a finger |
| **A four-digit PIN, if set** | So sign-offs are attributed to a person | Typed in |

**The app does not collect:** analytics, advertising identifiers, crash telemetry, contacts,
calendars, microphone audio, health data, or background location. There are no third-party
trackers or advertising SDKs of any kind.

## Where it is stored

**On your device.** By default everything stays in the app's own storage on that phone. Nothing
leaves it.

**On our server, only if you start or join a shared tracker.** If you choose "Start a shared
tracker", the record is stored on our behalf by **Supabase Inc.**, which hosts the database.
Everyone who has the eight-character join code can read and change that tracker's contents.
Treat the code like a key to the office: anyone you give it to can see the jobs, the photos and
the client details in it.

Supabase acts as our data processor. Their privacy terms: https://supabase.com/privacy

Our database region: **eu-west-1 (Ireland)**

## Location

Location is read **only** at the moment you tap "Use my location", and only while the app is open.
The app never tracks location in the background and never follows a phone's movement. What is
stored is a single pair of coordinates attached to a job or to the yard.

## Photographs

Photographs are reduced in size on the way in and stored with the job. They are not uploaded
anywhere except to a shared tracker, if you are using one. The app reads only the images you
explicitly pick or take; it does not scan your photo library.

## Who we share it with

Nobody, other than Supabase as our hosting provider. We do not sell data, we do not share it for
advertising, and we do not pass it to analytics companies. Documents you generate — job sheets and
handover certificates — leave the app only when *you* send them, using your phone's own share sheet.

## How long we keep it

Until it is deleted. Anyone using a tracker can delete a job, and "Erase everything" in Settings
clears the whole record from the device. To have a shared tracker deleted from our server, contact
us at the address above.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct
it, ask us to delete it, or object to our holding it. Write to the contact address above and we
will respond within one month. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
(ico.org.uk).

Our lawful basis for holding client and site details is **legitimate interest** — running a
scaffolding business and meeting the inspection-record duties that come with it.

## Children

The app is a work tool for scaffolders. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly
collect data from anyone under 16.

## Security

Data travels to our server over HTTPS. The database is not readable without a valid join code;
the underlying table is closed to direct access. However, because a join code alone grants access,
the security of a shared tracker rests on keeping that code within your firm.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and publish the new version at the same
address.
